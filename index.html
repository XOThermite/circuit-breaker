How it works

Tap a card when an exercise is done. Tap the big number to open the set counter dial — spin it, log sets as you go.

Hold a card for form tips with easier and harder variations. Timed holds ring a soft bell when done — two-sided stretches ding halfway to swap sides.

The circuit changes daily and quietly tunes each exercise to you over time. Pick your level below, shuffle if today's mix doesn't appeal, and split sets across the day however suits.

Work within your comfort — the easier variations are always legitimate, and if you have a health condition, check with your GP before starting.

Heads up: this viewer isn't running the app's code, so today's circuit can't be generated. On iPhone this usually happens when the file is opened as a Files / Quick Look preview — open it from the Claude app chat instead, or in Safari.
